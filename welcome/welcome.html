Dynamic placeholders

Smart templates that adapt to your work.

Create reusable prompts with dynamic inputs, pre-filled fallbacks, or automatic timestamps.

{{variable}}

Blank input field

Asks you for information each time you run the prompt. E.g. {{topic}}, {{customer_name}}, or {{code}}.

{{field|default}}

Pre-filled default value

Starts with your usual answer already typed. Hit Enter to keep it, or change it when needed. E.g. {{language|Python}} or {{tone|friendly}}.

{{date}}

Automatic today's date

Automatically inserts today's date using your computer's clock. Perfect for daily standups, journaling, or timestamped notes.
